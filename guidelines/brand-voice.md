# AI Scriptwriting Blueprint: High-Retention "What If" Videos

## 1. Core Structural Pillars
* **Cinematic Over Lecture:** Treat every script like a movie scene. Focus heavily on visual destruction, scale, and visceral impacts. Never teach dry math or physics formulas.
* **The Immortal Protagonist:** The main character (POV character) NEVER dies. Even if the universe explodes or atoms shatter, the character remains a physical observer, witnessing the madness with a smirk or a smile.
* **Lightning Pace:** Use brief, punchy sentence fragments. Every sentence must evoke an instantaneous, massive visual change. Keep total script length optimized for a 50-60 second video.

## 2. Audio & Sound FX Framework
* **Theme Song:** Always specify the background track as "Charlie's Inferno" by That Handsome Devil (to perfectly trigger the TikTok "Hussing" running meme style).
* **Custom SFX Cues:** Scripts must explicitly embed custom audio markers using bracket notation (e.g., `[SFX: Reality tearing distortion]`, `[SFX: Heavy sonic boom]`).

## 3. Asset & Modeling Directions
* **The Mundane Start:** Every video must start in a highly detailed, everyday location (e.g., Central Park, Manhattan downtown grid, a rooftop bar).
* **Stylized 3D Rendering:** Describe a mix of hyper-stylized 3D cartoon textures combined with chaotic physics particles (e.g., rigid objects warping into cones, light passing directly through transparent character meshes).
* **The Retention Loop Ending:** End every video by breaking the fourth wall, locking eyes with the camera, and asking a polarizing engagement question to drive comments.
